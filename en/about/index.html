About This Site

KAngel's Stream Room is an unofficial, fan-made interactive website based on NEEDY STREAMER OVERLOAD (also known as NEEDY GIRL OVERDOSE). It's a personal project, built and maintained by one person.

You can drop in to watch KAngel's stream, send messages in chat, or leave the page open and listen to her talk to herself. KAngel is powered by AI here, and her reactions can change with what's happening on stream and what people are saying in chat.

She might say something completely baffling now and then. There are still plenty of rough edges to work out (oops).

Anyway, if you're a fellow fan, you're more than welcome to hang out.

Why I Made This

I really love NEEDY STREAMER OVERLOAD.

It isn’t just KAngel. The art, the music, the story, and that wonderfully messy internet atmosphere are all reasons I keep coming back to the game.

I'd had this idea for ages: what if there were a KAngel stream you could actually visit and take part in? Not just a few pictures on a page or a set of prerecorded lines, but somewhere people could watch together, leave comments, and occasionally see something unexpected happen.

Of course, having an idea and actually making it are two very different things.

I'm not a professional game developer, and drawing and visual design aren't exactly my strengths. With some help from AI tools and what I know about programming, I've been putting this little site together one piece at a time.

It started out with a handful of simple interactions. More features have crept in since then, along with plenty of things I've rewritten, reworked, or thrown out after deciding they weren't such great ideas after all.

There's still a lot I'd like to improve. But seeing people come by to spend time here—and getting to know other NGO fans along the way—has made me really happy.

Updates

The site is still being worked on.

I've got more things I'd like to add, existing features to tidy up, and probably a few strange experiments I'll end up trying just because they sound interesting.

Since it's a one-person project, updates won't always happen on a regular schedule. I'll do my best to keep the server around for a long time, but I can't promise there won't be bugs or downtime. If you spot something broken, run into connection trouble, or catch KAngel saying something especially nonsensical, feel free to let me know.

Ideas are welcome, too. I can't promise I'll build every suggestion, but I do read them.
