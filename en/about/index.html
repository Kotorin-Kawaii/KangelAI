About the KAngel Live Room

The KAngel live room is an unofficial fan-made interactive project. Watch a virtual streamer and join the conversation through live chat.

About the project

The room supports live comments, emotional and behavioral expressions, and autonomous programs that can choose topics, speak across segments and observe feedback when a stage opportunity is available. The actual performance depends on live conditions.

Guests can watch and send ordinary comments. Signing in enables account features; some features and the classic room require an account.

FAQ

Is the streamer a real person?

No. The system generates the streamer’s replies, which may contain fictional or incorrect information.
